Privacy & enquiries

When you send an enquiry, The Crochet Center receives your name, the contact details you choose to share, your selected products and your message. These details are stored so the shop can respond and manage your request.

How your details are used

Your enquiry information is used to discuss your request, confirm details and follow up with you. It is not a marketing subscription. Please avoid including sensitive personal information or payment details in the form.

Your choices

You can enquire using your Instagram username, email address or WhatsApp number. To ask about, correct or request deletion of your enquiry information, contact @the_crochet_center_ on Instagram and include your enquiry reference if available. The shop will respond to your request and explain any information it needs to retain for an agreed order.

Saved selections

Your favourites and selection are stored locally in your browser. Clearing your browser’s site data removes them. The site uses a necessary, time-limited session cookie for signed-in shop administrators. There are no advertising or analytics trackers configured in this website.

External contact services

Instagram and, when enabled, WhatsApp open an external service. Information you share there is handled under that service’s own privacy terms.

Before an order is agreed

Sending an enquiry does not reserve stock, create a confirmed order or take payment. The shop will confirm product details, pricing, delivery, cancellation and return terms before you agree to an order. Payments are not collected through this website.
