THE LITTLE DETAILS

A stitch of clarity.

How do I order a piece?

Add your favourites to your selection and send an enquiry. You can also message us on Instagram. We’ll confirm availability, price and delivery before you place an order. Sending an enquiry does not place an order or take payment.

Can I choose my own colours?

We’d love to hear your idea. Use the custom enquiry form to describe your preferred colours, size or gift. We’ll let you know what is possible with the yarn and materials available.

How much does delivery cost?

Delivery options, charges and timing depend on your location and the piece you choose. These will be confirmed with your quote before an order is agreed.

Will my piece look exactly like the photo?

Every piece is handmade, so small variations are part of its charm. Colours can also look different across screens. Ask us for current photos or shade options when enquiring.

How do I care for crochet?

Care depends on the yarn, trims and construction. Ask for the care instructions for your chosen piece and keep delicate details away from rough handling.

What about changes, cancellations or returns?

Please discuss these with us before confirming your order, especially for personalised pieces. We’ll confirm the terms that apply to your piece in writing.
